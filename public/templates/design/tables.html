Tables

Tables and their variants, defined in table.css.

Basic

Applies to <table>. Add the table class to other elements. Header cells in <thead> have a background; <th> elsewhere is semibold without one. <caption> is placed below the table.

Variants

table-bordered draws a border around every cell. table-striped gives every second row a background, and table-hover highlights the row under the pointer. They can be combined.

Numeric cells

Cells with table-numeric are aligned to the end and use tabular figures, so digits line up.

Horizontal scrolling

Wrap a wide table in table-scroll to scroll the table instead of the page. The table is at least as wide as the container.
